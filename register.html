CS Theory
Why Nibbles Matter

A Nibble is exactly 4 bits. Because 2⁴ = 16, any 4-bit combination maps directly to one hexadecimal symbol (0 to F). This is why an 8-bit byte is always written as two hex characters (e.g. 0xA9)!

Two's Complement (Signed Mode)

In signed 8-bit math, the Most Significant Bit (b₇) is the Sign Bit with a negative weight of -128. All other bits (b⁸ to b₀) remain positive. That's why 11111111 is -128 + 127 = -1!

Bitwise Shifts

Shifting bits to the left (« 1) multiplies an integer by 2. Shifting to the right (» 1) performs integer floor division by 2. Hardware uses this instead of slow arithmetic multipliers!
